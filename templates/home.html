§ 01 · How it works
01

Upload and name the signers

Drop in a PDF and list who signs, in what order. Signature and date lines are found automatically; anything else you place with a click. You get a private management link the moment the envelope exists.

02

Sign in order

The first signer, usually the person who set it up, signs right away. Only then does the next signer get an email: their own link plus a copy carrying the signatures so far. Draw or type your signature; required boxes must be marked before you can sign.

03

Everyone gets the finished document

When the last signature lands, every party receives the signed PDF with a certificate page: who signed, when, from where, consent, and the document hashes. The audit trail lives on the management link.

§ 02 · Privacy

Nothing to browse

There is no index of documents, no search, no login. An envelope is reachable only through its management link and its signing links. Pages and files are served with no-store and no-index headers.

Legally sound

Each signer consents to sign electronically before signing (E-SIGN Act; Texas UETA). The certificate records consent, timestamps, IP addresses, and SHA-256 hashes of the original and signed documents.
